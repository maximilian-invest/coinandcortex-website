About Coin & Cortex

Coin & Cortex explains the psychology of money: why perfectly sensible people overpay, overspend and put off the boring stuff, and which small, practical systems help. We do it with hand-drawn doodle videos on YouTube and with longer, sourced articles on the blog.

Our recurring doodle character, Sam, stands in for all of us: smart enough to know better, human enough to do it anyway.

Editorial standards

  • Sources first. Every factual claim in an article is backed by a source we link at the end: peer-reviewed studies (usually via their DOI), official regulator or government publications, or established institutions. We prefer well-replicated findings over one-off headlines.
  • No invented numbers. We never make up statistics, studies, quotes or experts. Example numbers are labelled as examples. If a famous claim is contested or a quote is probably misattributed, we say so.
  • Written to last. We avoid time-bound statistics, current prices and "deal of the month" content, so an article stays useful for years. Where specific tools are useful, they go in a clearly marked, swappable appendix, never as paid placements.
  • Practical, not preachy. Each article should leave you with something concrete to do. We describe general habits, not personal financial advice.
  • Corrections. If you spot an error or a broken source, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We check every report and fix confirmed errors in the article.

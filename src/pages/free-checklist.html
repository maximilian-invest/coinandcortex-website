What's in it

Six short moves you can run in a few minutes before you renew, sign up, or let a contract roll over — so the price you pay is the fair one, not the one picked for you.

  1. Set your number before you open the app.
  2. Do the second-screen check.
  3. Trust the price history, not the sale badge.
  4. Hold a Switching Day, once a year.
  5. Give your AI shopper rules.
  6. Look for the label, and report it.

Page two walks you through your first Switching Day in about 30 minutes.

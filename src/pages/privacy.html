Hosting and server logs

This is a static website. The hosting provider ([hosting provider, to be filled in], a static host such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages) automatically processes technical data in its server logs — including your IP address — in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure and stable. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and runs no tracking or advertising scripts. There is no third-party content embedded in the pages (no video embeds, no web fonts loaded from other servers, no social widgets).

Links to YouTube and other sites

Links to our YouTube channel and videos are plain links. We do not embed YouTube on this site, so YouTube only receives data about you after you click and leave for youtube.com. YouTube's own privacy policy then applies.

Payments via Stripe

We do not sell directly on this website. When you choose to buy a product, you are redirected to a checkout page operated by Stripe. Stripe processes your payment data (for example your name, email address, billing country and order details) as an independent controller and/or processor. We receive from Stripe your name, email address, billing country and order data so we can complete the purchase and meet our tax obligations.

The legal basis for processing this data is the performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for the retention of accounting records, our legal obligations under Austrian tax law (retention for seven years pursuant to § 132 BAO). You can read Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Contacting us by email

If you email us, we process the data you send (your email address and the content of your message) to handle your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or the performance of a contract where your message concerns an order.

Newsletter

There is no newsletter at the moment. If we introduce one in the future, it will only be sent with your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and you will be able to withdraw that consent at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data and to its rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future.

Right to complain

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

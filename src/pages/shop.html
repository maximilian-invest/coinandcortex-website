Questions

Does this work in my country?

The kit is written for the US, the UK and Europe and stays general on purpose. The routine, the tracker and the scripts work wherever you pay for contracts and subscriptions. Cancellation rules and notice periods differ by country and provider, so the kit shows you where to check your own.

Is this financial or legal advice?

No. It's an organisation and education toolkit. You decide what to keep, switch or cancel, and you should always check your own contract terms.

Do I need Excel?

No. There's a version for Google Sheets (free with a Google account), a version for Excel or LibreOffice, a CSV, and a printable tracker page in the PDF.

How long does it take?

Set-up takes about 20–30 minutes the first time. After that, one Switching Day of 30–60 minutes a year plus an optional 15-minute monthly check.

I hate phone calls. Will this still help?

Yes. Every script has a chat/email version, and many changes can be done online. The scripts are short, polite and come with lines for when the agent says no.

Will it go out of date?

It's built around principles, routines and templates, not this month's deals. The only part that names specific apps or websites is a short "Tool examples (may change)" appendix.

Which calendar apps work with the .ics file?

Most of them. Common calendar apps on phones and computers can import .ics files. Some apps ignore the built-in alerts, so set your own reminder if you don't see one.

Can I share it with friends?

The licence is for personal use by the buyer and their household. If it helped, send friends the free Fair Price Protocol checklist or the video instead.
